§ PRIVACIA

The mirror is local.

Last updated: 3 May 2026

Übermensch is a self-tracking iOS app that records what you choose to write down — body data, expenses, journal entries, daily checks, philosophical reflections — and shows it back to you. The mirror lives on your device. Nothing leaves it.

What we collect

Nothing. Übermensch does not have a backend. No accounts, no sign-in, no email collection, no telemetry, no analytics. The app does not transmit your records to any server we control — because we do not run any server.

What stays on your device

Everything you create or import:

  • Journal entries, daily check-ins, body sensations, identity statements
  • HealthKit data (steps, sleep, heart rate, blood pressure) — only if you grant permission
  • Photos attached to journal entries
  • Voice recordings transcribed for journal entries (audio is processed and discarded; only the transcript is saved)
  • Imported books, lab results, expenses, income, recurring transaction templates, cycle data
  • AI dialog history — stored as memory entries, never sent to a third party

This data is encrypted at rest by iOS using the device's standard data protection. It is included in your device backup if you enable iCloud backups for the app, but Übermensch itself does not initiate any cloud sync.

HealthKit

If you grant HealthKit permission, Übermensch reads basic, non-clinical metrics (steps, weight, sleep duration, resting heart rate, blood pressure) for display, and writes back values you enter manually inside the app. HealthKit data is never used for advertising, never sold, never sent off-device, and never used to give medical advice. The body map labels readings as "signal," not "diagnosis." A medical disclaimer accompanies every region detail.

On-device AI

Übermensch supports two on-device AI providers:

  • Apple Foundation Models (iOS 26+) — Apple's framework. Inference runs on-device.
  • Phi-4 Mini Instruct — a 2.4 GB language model from Microsoft, distributed via HuggingFace. The model is downloaded only when you tap "Download" in Profile → AI Providers. Once downloaded, all inference is local.

Your prompts and dialog history are processed entirely on the device. They are not sent to OpenAI, Anthropic, Microsoft, Apple, or any other third party for inference.

When the app does talk to the network

Two limited cases, both initiated by your explicit action:

  • Open Food Facts — when you search for a food in the calorie sheet, the app sends your text query to openfoodfacts.org to retrieve nutritional info. No personal data is sent; only the search term.
  • HuggingFace — when you tap "Download" for the Phi-4 model, the app downloads the model weights from huggingface.co. This is an anonymous file download.

If you never search for food and never download the local AI model, the app makes no network requests at all.

What we do NOT do

  • No advertising. No ad-network SDKs.
  • No third-party analytics (no Firebase, no Mixpanel, no Amplitude, no Sentry).
  • No social media SDKs.
  • No location tracking.
  • No contacts or address-book access.
  • No data sharing with anyone.
  • No fingerprinting.

Children

Übermensch is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from anyone of any age (because it does not collect data from anyone).

Your rights and controls

  • See what's stored — Profile → Privacy panel lists every category of on-device data with size and last-touched date.
  • Export — Profile → Export creates a Markdown or JSON dump of all your records.
  • Delete — Profile → Privacy → Wipe all clears every locally-stored record.
  • Revoke HealthKit — iOS Settings → Health → Übermensch.
  • Revoke notifications — iOS Settings → Notifications → Übermensch.
  • Uninstall — deleting the app removes all of its on-device data.

Privacy Manifest

The app ships with a PrivacyInfo.xcprivacy declaring: tracking = false, no collected data types, and the standard Apple "required reason" API categories the app uses (UserDefaults for app group, file timestamps for photo display, system boot time for elapsed-time measurement, disk space for warning before model downloads).

Changes to this policy

If we ever change how the app handles data, this page will be updated and the change will be reflected in the app's release notes.
